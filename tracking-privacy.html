Website privacy & measurement

Last updated: 30 October 2026

This notice describes measurement on beyondbelief.ai, operated by Beyond Belief. The Android app has a separate privacy policy.

Your choice

We ask before loading Google Ads measurement. If you reject or have not chosen, we do not load the Google tag or send measurement events to Google. You can change your choice using “Cookie settings” in the landing page footer. Your choice is stored locally in your browser until you clear it or change it.

Google Ads measurement

If you allow measurement, Google receives browser and device information, the page URL (which may contain an advertising click identifier), and an event when you click a link to our app on Google Play. Google Ads may set first-party advertising measurement cookies such as _gcl_aw, generally lasting up to 90 days. These help attribute a store visit to an ad. A click does not confirm an installation or purchase.

We keep advertising personalisation, Google signals and enhanced conversions disabled in this website implementation. We do not ask for names, email addresses or other form data on this landing page. Rejecting measurement later stops new conversion events and removes accessible Google click measurement cookies from this site.

For Google's processing and retention, see Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from partner sites.

Google Play links

Our store links contain a campaign referrer identifying this website as the source. Following a link opens Google Play, whose own privacy terms apply.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact contact@beyondbelief.ai.
