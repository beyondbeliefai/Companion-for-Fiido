This notice describes beyondbelief.ai, operated by Beyond Belief. The Android app has a separate privacy policy.

No website analytics

This landing page does not load Google Ads or Analytics tracking scripts, send click-conversion events, or set analytics cookies. The previous optional measurement feature has been removed. On returning visits, the page removes its old measurement consent preference and accessible Google click-measurement cookies.

Google Play links

Links to the app contain a fixed campaign referrer identifying this website as their source. It is the same for all visitors, contains no unique visitor or advertising click identifier, and may be used in aggregated Google Play reports. This website does not receive an individual visitor's installation status. Following a link opens Google Play, whose own privacy terms apply.
